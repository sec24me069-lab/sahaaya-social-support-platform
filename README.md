SAHAAYA
AI-Powered Social Support & Civic Assistance Platform
Technology that stands with people.

SAHAAYA is an AI-powered social and civic support platform designed to help individuals understand social and civic concerns, discover relevant support services, and identify appropriate next steps.
The platform focuses on improving accessibility to information and support for women, underserved communities, and individuals who may face language, awareness, or digital-access barriers.
🌍 Problem
People facing social and civic problems often struggle to identify:
- Where to seek help
- Which service or authority is relevant
- What action should be taken
- Which support resources are available
- How to access information in a simple and understandable way
Information is often distributed across different departments, helplines, websites, and organizations. This fragmentation can create confusion and delay meaningful action.
SAHAAYA aims to bridge this information and accessibility gap.
💡 Solution
SAHAAYA provides an AI-assisted interface through which users can describe their concern in natural language.
The system is designed to:
1. Understand the user's concern
2. Identify the relevant support category
3. Match the concern with available resources
4. Provide clear guidance
5. Suggest appropriate support or referral pathways
6. Help the user identify the next actionable step
🎯 Target Users
SAHAAYA is designed with particular consideration for:
- Women seeking social or civic support
- Underserved communities
- Individuals with limited awareness of available services
- Users facing language or digital-access barriers
- Citizens seeking guidance for civic and social concerns
🚀 Key Features
- AI-assisted conversational support
- Social and civic issue identification
- Support and service discovery
- Guidance and referral pathways
- Multilingual accessibility

- User-oriented action guidance
- Scalable civic-support architecture

⚠️ Responsible Use
SAHAAYA is intended to provide informational guidance and support navigation.
It does not replace emergency services, professional legal advice, medical care, or official authorities.
Critical situations should always be directed to appropriate official emergency or support channels.
